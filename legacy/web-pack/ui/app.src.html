What's in the pack

36 construction kits for UK garage, garage house, minimal tech house, speed garage, classic house, groove house and tech house, each with drum, bass, chord and melody MIDI, a full song and an extended mix, plus 0 vocals, 0 bass and keys sounds, 0 loops, fills and FX and 0 drum hits. Everything is 128–140 BPM with its key and Camelot code.

Play a kit, then audition anything over it: one-shots start on the next beat, loops on the next bar, and drum loops follow the kit's tempo. Fits narrows each list to keys and tempos that mix with the kit. Star sounds to collect them in your Crate. The Sound lab resamples any sound in the pack, or one of yours, through pitch, filter, crush, gate, echo and reverb, and saves the result as a WAV. The Halloween tab holds a seasonal set: spooky vocals, FX and loops on their own pads.

MIDI

Four-bar regions at 480 PPQ with tempo, 4/4 and key signature written in. Swing is baked into the note timing, so the groove survives the drag into your DAW. Each kit also has an 8-bar arrangement, a full song (seven tracks with section markers, roll-and-riser builds, CC74/CC11 automation and a new breakdown progression) and a 6 to 8 minute extended mix: 16 tracks with the drums split into kick, snare, hats, percussion and fills, plus stabs, counter-melody, vocal-chop triggers and FX hits (note 36 impact, 38 reverse cymbal, 40 downlifter, 41 sub drop). The kits marked New layers add a vocal-guide track with each word as a MIDI lyric, a shouts track for the pack's ad-libs, break slices, acid, donk, hoover, siren, talkbox, strings, brass, choir, organ and piano layers, pitch-bend moves for a ±12-semitone bend range, and a sidechain trigger. Changing the key or tempo changes the files you save.

Audio

44.1 kHz / 16-bit WAV. Loops, fills and FX are cut to whole bars and fold their tails back onto the start, so they repeat cleanly. The slice-ready break is one straight bar: cut it into 16 equal slices and it lines up with the Break Slices track in UKG07. Sung hooks are 4-bar loops locked to their kit's tempo, swing and key. Spoken vocals have no fixed pitch and fit any key; their BPM is the tempo their echoes are timed to. Files arrive as .zip because this viewer can only save zip archives.
