# Four first-listen kits (round 2)

After round 1 you asked for four kits: one per direction. This plan widens S1a from one kit to four first-listen kits. Each kit is a set of 12 sounds and a 4-bar context loop, dry and wet, like FUT01's S1a set, rendered and checked the same way. FUT01 also gets its fix round from your round 1 notes. All four come back to you together as listening round 2. ADR 0010 records the change of scope; nothing after S1a changes yet.

## What round 1 taught us (binding on every kit)

1. **Kick: the split design.** You picked A "Split" (Cool 4): a clean sub, a short knock above about 110 Hz, and grit only on top. B "Tight" sounded "a bit hard like its really slamming the limiter". Every kick keeps its transient and dynamics, and a new crest floor checks it (ADR 0011).
2. **Bass must be musical and make you dance.** The reese was the weakest sound (Cool 2, "needs the most work of all"; in the loop it "doesnt make me want to dance"). Two fixes go into every kit:
   - **The line.** An original bassline that grooves against the kick instead of following it:
     - It leaves room on the kick and answers it in the gaps.
     - It has a melodic contour in the key (root, fifth, minor seventh, octave, approach notes) and one motif that comes back with a variation.
     - It mixes short and held notes, with ghost notes and an occasional slide.
     - Every lane uses the kit's one swing.
   - **The sound.** A bass with body you can hear on small speakers, plus movement (filter, vowel or pitch). Every kit's bass comes as A **clean** (the strict mud gate, as in round 1) and B **warm** (the proposed bass-body rule, ADR 0011). You decide between them by ear.
3. **Melodic tops are the strength.** The chrome pluck got Cool 5, and in the loop "the high plucks are nice". Every kit has a signature melodic element with an original phrase, placed up front in its loop.
4. **Vocal lines sound like real club vocals.** The sung chops scored 4, but the line was a "weird phrase".
   - New lines are short (3–6 words), plain and singable, and say something direct.
   - Each is checked against existing song titles and lyrics before it's sung.
   - Round 2 uses clean lines only.
5. **Shots and fills make a musical gesture.** The growl and the glitch fill got 3. Every shot or fill is tempo-synced, pitched to the key and has a clear shape: a rhythm, a vowel move or a pitch move, not just texture.
6. **No mud, still.** Kicks, toms and booms keep the strict gate unchanged. Pads, stabs and textures are high-passed at about 100 Hz and mono below 150 Hz.

## The four kits

Each kit has its own DNA in its kit module, so its sounds read as one family and differ from the other kits. The DNA is:
- one inharmonic ratio set
- one Kokoro voice blend
- one space seed
- the root, BPM and swing

### FUT01 "Alloy Signal": the fix round
- **Bass:** the reese is rebuilt as A clean and B warm. The fix round also adds three original 2-bar basslines (kick, hat and bass) that groove against the 2-step kick, so you can pick one. The context loop uses the designer's pick, marked as such.
- **Growl:** two tempo-synced moves pitched to F#, with an audible vowel rhythm.
- **Glitch fill:** rebuilt from the kit's own sounds, tempo-synced and pitched to the key.
- **Sung chops:** three candidate lines, title-checked, each sung once. The chops are cut from the best one, and Whisper checks it.
- **Kick:** A (Split) stays. B (Tight) leaves the set.
- **Unchanged:** snare, hat and alloy hit wait for your ratings. The pad, the pluck and its phrase, the robot and the riser stay too.
- **Context loop:** the new bassline; everything else stays, the plucks included.

### FUT02 "Orbit Relay": sci-fi club
"Orbital" DNA: near-harmonic, slightly detuned chrome-synth ratios, plus a laser pitch-drop gesture shared by its perc, fill and riser.
1. **Kick:** split design, punchy, the sub gone before the next beat.
2. **Clap:** an electro clap layered with a short snare.
3. **Hats:** closed 16ths and an open offbeat hat that the closed one chokes.
4. **Zap:** a pitch-dropping laser perc, tuned to the key.
5. **Bass:** a rolling offbeat synth bass (A clean / B warm), plus a 2-bar rolling line in D minor.
6. **Drop bass:** a gliding bass hit for transitions.
7. **Starfield pad:** a wide chord, Dm9, with slow filter movement.
8. **Arp lead:** an original 2-bar 16th arpeggio, plus multisample notes.
9. **Sung chops:** a new line, female voice blend.
10. **Vocoder phrase:** male, sliced to the grid.
11. **Laser fill:** one bar.
12. **Riser:** 8 bars, noise plus a laser rise, landing on the downbeat.

### FUT03 "Glass Current": hyper-clean future house
"Glass" DNA: clean bell and glass partials, phase-locked layers, and a sidechain pump in the loop.
1. **Kick:** tight and clean, a short sub, a clear click. It must pass the crest floor.
2. **Clap:** three phase-locked layers, snappy.
3. **Hats:** a shaker or closed 16ths, plus an open offbeat hat.
4. **Metal pling:** a pitched metallic perc.
5. **Bass:** the bouncy metallic house bass that dips in pitch on each note (A clean / B warm), plus a 2-bar line in G minor with octave jumps.
6. **Vowel bass:** two shots that move between vowels.
7. **Chord stab:** a supersaw stab, Gm9, short and sidechained.
8. **Pluck lead:** bright and clean, with an original 2-bar phrase.
9. **Sung chops:** a new line, a bright female blend, pitched.
10. **Vocoder phrase:** a "drop" phrase.
11. **Build fill:** a one-bar snare or tom roll.
12. **Riser:** 8 bars, noise and pitch, plus an impact.

### FUT04 "Furnace Code": dark industrial / techno
"Iron" DNA: heavy plate and anvil modal ratios, a dark concrete space, and a low male voice blend.
1. **Kick:** the 8× oversampled chain. Distortion stays above about 300 Hz, so the sub stays clean (split design).
2. **Snare:** an industrial metal snare or clap, gated.
3. **Hats:** dark metallic 16ths, plus an open metal hat or ride.
4. **Anvil:** a pitched clank on the iron ratios.
5. **Acid bass:** a resonant line with accents and slides (A clean / B warm), plus a 2-bar original 16th acid pattern in E minor.
6. **Rumble:** a 2-bar kick-fed low rumble. It must pass the loop gap checks: nothing in 150–400 Hz before the next kick.
7. **Drone:** a dark pad or texture, Em(add9), high-passed.
8. **Modular blips:** an original 2-bar sequence.
9. **Sung chops:** a new, darker line.
10. **Robot phrase:** distorted, male, sliced to the grid.
11. **Stutter fill:** metallic noise, one bar.
12. **Riser:** 8 bars of noise with metallic resonances, landing on the downbeat.

## How each kit is made

- **One kit, one recipe package.** Each kit is a kit module and a recipe package of its own. Shared tools (DSP, QA, rendering, sends, the vocal chain) are shared code that no kit designer edits.
- **Checks.** QA as in ADR 0009, plus ADR 0011's kick crest floor and, on the B basses, the bass-body rule. The same bytes on every render (ADR 0007).
- **What you get per kit:**
  - FLAC masters and WAV copies
  - a `LISTEN.md`
  - the context loop dry, wet and low end only
- **Round 2.** One listening page covers all four kits and saves your ratings for each, as round 1's did.

**The round 2 gate**, per kit: the S1a gate. At least 8 of 12 sounds kept with Cool ≥ 4 after one fix round, a median Cool ≥ 4, and "one kit?" yes on at least 10.

**After round 2:** the kits that pass go on to their full render (S1c, per kit). Which ones, and in what order, is your call then.
