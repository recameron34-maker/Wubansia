## 2. v0.2 Samples drawer (ADR 0024)
The **Samples** button opens a drawer of every kit's sounds in seven categories, Accents first (35, 37, 36 and 32
sounds; all 618 files reachable through each sound's File box). A click plays the file dry at -6 dB over the groove;
a drag copies the kit's own 48 kHz / 24-bit WAV, byte for byte and checked against kit.json's SHA-256, into
`~/Music/Wubansia/Samples/<KIT> <Title>/<sound> - <KIT> <BPM> <Key> <Camelot>.wav` and hands it to the DAW. That
folder is never cleaned up (Live references dropped audio where it sits). No new parameter or saved state; offline
bounces are unchanged. Spec: `docs/plans/v0.2-samples-spec.md`; user docs: `dist/INSTALL.md` "Samples into your DAW".
